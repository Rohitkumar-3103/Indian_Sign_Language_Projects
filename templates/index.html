ISL Hand Guide

Two-Handed vs Single-Handed

Unlike ASL, Indian Sign Language features extensive two-handed gestures:

  • Green Skeleton: Right / Dominant Hand
  • Cyan Skeleton: Left / Secondary Hand
  • Vowels A, E, I, O, U use right index touching left fingertips!
